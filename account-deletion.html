Family Hub

Account Deletion Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Family Hub account and what happens to information associated with it.

1. How to request deletion

You can request deletion in either of these ways:

  • Within the app: Open Account and choose "Delete my account and household", where available.
  • Without signing in: Email Kevinaflanigan@gmail.com with the subject "Family Hub account deletion request".

Your email should include the email address associated with your Family Hub account. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Request account deletion by email

2. What happens when you request deletion?

We will review your request, verify your identity where necessary and explain any steps needed to complete deletion.

Deletion may include:

  • Your Family Hub sign-in account and associated account profile.
  • Your private to-do items.
  • Your household membership and join-request information.
  • Shared household information, where you are the sole household owner and the household can be safely deleted.
  • Information stored locally by the app, where it can be identified and removed.

3. Shared household information

Family Hub supports shared household accounts. Deleting one person's account must not automatically delete information belonging to other household members.

If other members remain in your household, or you are the household owner, we may need to resolve household membership or ownership before completing deletion. We will explain the available steps when handling your request.

The current in-app deletion function only completes household deletion when the account holder is the sole household member and owner. Other circumstances require assistance through the contact email above.

4. What happens to other household members?

Where a household remains active, shared household information may continue to be available to its remaining authorised members. Your individual account and private information will be handled separately from the household's shared information.

5. How long does deletion take?

We aim to respond to deletion requests within 30 days. If a request is complex or requires additional verification, we will explain any delay.

6. Information we may need to retain

In limited circumstances, we may need to retain certain information where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary and protected appropriately.

7. Does uninstalling the app delete my account?

No. Removing the app or clearing your browser does not necessarily delete your Family Hub account or information stored in Firebase. You must use the in-app deletion option or submit a deletion request by email.
