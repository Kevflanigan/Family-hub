Family Hub

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Family Hub collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use the Family Hub website and application.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Family Hub is operated by its owner, who is responsible for the personal information processed through the service.

For privacy questions or requests, contact: Kevinaflanigan@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Family Hub, information may include:

  • Account information: your email address, display name and Firebase user identifier.
  • Household information: household name and identifier, membership details, roles, permissions and join requests.
  • Shared household information: planner entries such as calendar events, meals, bills, budgets, shopping lists, tasks, chores, addresses, places and family ideas.
  • Private information: items you add to your private to-do list, which is stored separately under your account.
  • Device and browser information: certain preferences and planner information stored locally in your browser to support the application.
  • Location-related information: postcodes you enter for saved places may be sent to a postcode lookup service to obtain map coordinates.

3. How we use your information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Connect you to your household and manage membership.
  • Display and synchronise household information between authorised members.
  • Keep your private to-do items separate from shared household information.
  • Provide postcode lookup and map features.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and protect the service.
  • Respond to support, privacy and account deletion requests.

4. Shared household information

Family Hub allows people to share information within a household. Information saved in shared household sections may be visible to other authorised household members, depending on their roles and permissions.

Your private to-do list is stored separately under your individual account and is not intended to be visible to other household members.

Only add information to shared sections that you are comfortable sharing with your household.

5. Where information is stored

Family Hub uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to manage accounts and store household and private information.

Some planner preferences and information may also be stored in your browser's local storage. This means information stored locally may remain on that device until it is cleared.

6. Third-party services

Family Hub relies on services provided by other organisations to operate certain features. These include:

  • Google Firebase: account authentication and database storage.
  • Postcodes.io: postcode lookup for map locations.
  • OpenStreetMap: map data and map tiles.
  • Third-party content delivery services: delivery of software libraries and some images used by the application.

Those providers process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy information. Their services may process information outside the United Kingdom.

7. Legal basis for processing

Where UK data protection law applies, personal information is processed where necessary to provide the Family Hub service, to pursue legitimate interests such as maintaining and securing the service, or where another lawful basis applies.

8. How long we keep information

We keep account and household information while it is needed to provide the service. If an account deletion request is received, we will take steps to delete the relevant personal information, subject to any legal obligations or legitimate reasons to retain limited information.

Information stored in your browser may need to be cleared separately from the device.

9. Keeping information secure

We use Firebase authentication and database access controls to help protect account and household information. You should keep your password confidential and sign out when using a shared device.

No internet service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request a copy of information you provided in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, email Kevinaflanigan@gmail.com.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

11. Children's information

Family Hub is a household planning tool. Adults should take care when entering information about children or other family members and should avoid adding unnecessary sensitive personal information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Family Hub develops. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
